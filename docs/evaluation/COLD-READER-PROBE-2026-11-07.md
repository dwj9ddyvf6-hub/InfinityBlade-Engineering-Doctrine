# A fresh-reader probe of the two ascent frameworks

A separate reader started without the implementation conversation and received five source-inspection questions. It used the in-progress Test and InfinityBlade working trees, beginning at their entry documents. This was one bounded navigation and reasoning probe, with no baseline treatment, runtime execution or model-performance estimate. Its private record retained actual read ranges, source links and 36 file hashes; the then-current HEADs alone did not identify uncommitted candidate bytes.

| Question | Observed result | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| What different jobs do the repositories perform, and where should a reader begin? | Identified Test's domain library and Doctrine's operating discipline; followed entry, curriculum and exact-source routes. | Those selected entry routes were usable by a fresh reader. |
| How does full reading resume across contexts and cover opaque artifacts? | Explained pinned identity, private ledger, actual receipt acknowledgement, format-aware review and explicit unread portions. | The reader could explain the coverage contract and distinguish it from comprehension. |
| Which rule governs the supplied Teddy ORB stop/target example? | Located the frozen spec and `_stop_target`; derived 99.90 stop, 1.10 risk and 102 target. | Source retrieval and consistency. The exact example was already taught, so this is not a held-out generalization result. |
| What happens after provider acceptance followed by timeout or crash? | Located `EffectQueue.reconcile`, outbound attempt reconciliation and Test's persistent-simulator process drill; distinguished external intent identity from local generation. | The reader found concrete implementation and verification routes. It read those tests; it did not execute them. |
| What useful transfer could this framework support? | Proposed a revision-aware annotation dispatcher with alternative approaches and a predeclared failure experiment. | A fresh proposal produced during this probe; its engineering benefit remains to be tested. |

The proposal binds annotation intent to dataset content, sample, taxonomy and destination identity, then reconciles uncertain provider effects under that intent. Its experiment compares blind retries, sample-only deduplication and revision-bound reconciliation under acceptance crashes, changed taxonomies, stale absence and delayed callbacks. It explicitly retains an unresolved outcome when the receiver supplies neither authoritative lookup nor idempotency. This illustrates a transfer from source-variant reasoning and durable execution into another domain; it is not claimed as novel in the literature or already implemented here.

The probe exposed a real clarification need: Test's standing integration requirement calls for hard deadlines, while its reference thread helper supplies cooperative waiting limits. The final release clarified this in [chapter 18](https://github.com/dwj9ddyvf6-hub/Test/blob/main/agentic-trade-desk/docs/18_recovery_and_fault_drills.md): hard deadlines remain mandatory at deployment, and an interruptible transport or enforced process boundary is still required. The probe also exposed a practical reading trap during development: default `HEAD` examples read committed objects, not new working files. Candidate studies must bind the actual source being inspected; the published reader contract remains pinned to commits.

Several initial large reads truncated. The reader repaired the cited portions with bounded reads and retained partial coverage for unfinished files. It did not claim a complete repository reading or acknowledge unseen chunks. This is a desirable use of the reading protocol, while also showing why a full-reading claim needs its separate ledger.

Use this result as feedback on the inspected routes and exercises. To measure improvement caused by the documentation, run the [paired behavioral protocol](../BEHAVIOR-EVALUATION.md) on independent fresh tasks with fixed model/host/tool/budget conditions, both treatments, multiple trials and an untouched final set. Keep regressions and cost visible. This probe supplies no universal or cross-model uplift percentage.
